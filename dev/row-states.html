Table row backgrounds

Zebra is the quietest step by design - it helps the eye track across a wide row, nothing more. Hover and selection sit at a similar weight (about 1.14 above the row in light) and are told apart by hue, not by loudness: indigo is transient, green is identity. Hovering a selected row stacks both. The code cards matter too: they sit inside these cells, so their separation from the row has to survive every row state.
